This chapter is about what the public believes about the central bank. The public cannot see which inflation rate we really aim at, so it infers our aim from the policy rates we set: a rate higher than it expected suggests that we aim at lower inflation, and a lower rate suggests the opposite. What the public believes about our aim shapes the inflation it expects, and the inflation it expects shapes the inflation we get today. Later in this chapter you will also be able to announce the policy rates you plan to set.
