In this last chapter, everything you have met so far is switched on at once, and nothing is scripted any more: changes in spending, costs, productivity and government spending arrive at random, some small and some large, and some situations from earlier chapters return in a new form. The staff's selection in the newspaper marks the articles that matter most for your decision. Read them first, watch what the public expects and believes, keep your announcements where you can, and decide.
