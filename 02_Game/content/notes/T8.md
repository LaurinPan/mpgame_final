This topic is about all seven earlier topics at once. The mechanisms of Chapters A to D act together. Shocks arrive at random, productivity shocks appear for the first time, and situations from earlier chapters return.
Typical mistakes, each with a hint:
1. Reacting to a small shock as if it were large. Hint: the size the newspaper reports.
2. Forgetting the announcement in a busy quarter. Hint: the record judges every announced quarter.
3. Treating a productivity shock like a demand shock. Hint: whether inflation and output move in the same or in opposite directions.
