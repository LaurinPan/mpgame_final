# TOUR-T1

The walk-through at the start of Topic 1 (Section 4.3.2). Each heading names the panel the step points at; the text is the step's.

## figures
These figures and charts show last quarter's economy as the statistics office reports it, its output and trend, inflation, wage inflation and unemployment, and how many people live and work here.

## rate
This is your instrument, the policy rate, the short-term interest rate the central bank sets. It cannot fall below zero or rise above 20 percent.

## chat
This is the chat with the bank's Chief Economist. He briefs you whenever something happens and answers your questions.
